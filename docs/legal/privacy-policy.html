Privacy Policy

Application: Nyx Creator Dashboard

Last Updated: July 2026

Nyx Creator Dashboard is a personal creator management application used to manage Instagram content, review engagement and generate AI-assisted replies.

Information Collected

After authorization through Meta, the application may access:

  • Instagram profile information
  • Published media (posts, reels and videos)
  • Comments associated with published media
  • Messaging data (future functionality)
  • Basic analytics provided by the Instagram Platform

Purpose of Collection

The information is used solely to:

  • Synchronize Instagram content.
  • Display creator analytics.
  • Generate AI-assisted reply drafts.
  • Allow the account owner to manage community engagement.

Data Storage

Data is stored locally on the developer's own infrastructure for personal use. No user data is sold, rented or shared with third parties.

Third Party Services

This application communicates with the official Meta Instagram Platform APIs. No other third-party advertising or tracking services are used.

Data Security

Reasonable security measures are used to protect locally stored data and access tokens.

Your Rights

You may revoke this application's access at any time from your Meta account settings or request deletion of stored data by contacting the developer.
